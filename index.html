          💡 Security Tips

          🔐

          Screen Lock

          Always use a PIN, pattern, password, or biometric lock on your device. This is your first line of defense if it's lost or stolen.

          🔄

          OS Updates

          Install operating system updates promptly. They frequently patch serious security vulnerabilities attackers actively exploit.

          👁️

          App Permissions

          Review what permissions apps request (camera, mic, location). Deny anything that doesn't make sense for the app's purpose.

          🏪

          Official App Stores

          Only install apps from Google Play or trusted official sources. Sideloaded apps bypass critical security scanning.

          🔑

          Two-Factor Authentication

          Enable 2FA wherever offered. Even if your password leaks, attackers still can't get in without your second factor.

          🛡️

          VPN on Public Wi-Fi

          Public Wi-Fi networks can be monitored by others on the same network. A VPN encrypts your traffic to prevent snooping.

          💾

          Backups

          Regularly back up important data. This protects you against ransomware, device loss, and accidental deletion.

          ⚠️

          Skepticism of Links

          Be suspicious of unexpected links in emails, texts, or QR codes — even from people you know. Verify before clicking.
